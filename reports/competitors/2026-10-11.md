## Patterns worth testing

- **Lead the news day with the biggest national breaking story (storms, disasters, deaths of famous people).** Evidence: The hurricane Reel: 730 plays in about 7.5 hours. Same batch: press secretary 10, dog rescue 3, teen helper 1. Yesterday's Nobel Reel: 152. Test: For 5 days mark each Reel hard-breaking, politics or good news; compare median plays per group after 24 hours.
- **Stop treating scheduling as the cause of low plays on Unpopular Truths.** Evidence: 5 hand-posted Reels on Oct 10: 0 plays after 20 hours. 6 scheduled that day: 29. Oct 7's three hand-posted also got 0. Test: Keep the open test to Oct 16 as planned, but add the volume test below so both can be read.
- **Vary the look from Reel to Reel.** Evidence: Meta (Mar 13 2026) pushes down content it classes as unoriginal; INMA names 'same template across posts' as a demonetisation trigger. All our Reels use one layout. Test: Rotate 3 clearly different layouts (text position, font, footage type) for 5 days; compare plays per layout.
- **Hold posting volume steady; no jumps.** Evidence: INMA lists sudden volume swings as a trigger. We went from 3 a day (Oct 6 to 8) to 8 to 11 a day (Oct 9 and 10), and plays fell from hundreds to single digits on the same days. Test: 3 a day for 3 days, then compare median plays per Reel with Oct 9 and 10.
- **Keep image posts alongside Reels on both pages.** Evidence: Socialinsider (872,075 posts, Jan 2025 to Aug 2026, brand pages): reach rate images 1.20%, albums 1.15%, Reels 0.55%, links 0.20%. Buffer: photos have the highest median engagement. Test: One still image a day per page for 5 days; compare reach and reactions with that day's Reels.

## What to upgrade on Unpopular Truths

1. **Cut to 3 Reels a day for 3 days and hold it steady.** Evidence: 11 Reels on Oct 10 got 29 plays; 3 a day on Oct 6 and 8 got 140 to 587 on five of six. INMA lists sudden volume swings as a trigger. Posting by hand at the same volume still got 0. Test: Oct 12 to 14: 3 a day. Compare median plays per Reel with Oct 9 and 10.
2. **Use the plan's question as the caption every time.** Evidence: Three of the five hand-posted Reels went out with only a hashtag as caption. All five have 0 plays, so it cannot be judged alone, but it removes the comment prompt. Test: Keep as a rule; it also lets the report match Reels to the plan.
3. **Rotate 3 clearly different layouts.** Evidence: Meta (Mar 2026) pushes down content it classes as unoriginal; INMA: same template across posts is a trigger; Fanpage Karma (2026): repetitive posts lose reach over time. Test: 5 days, one layout per Reel in turn; compare plays per layout.
4. **Set a 12-second minimum and hold the second line to second 4 or 5.** Evidence: Meta (Mar 30 2026): views under 5 seconds do not count for earnings and videos under 10 seconds earn nothing. Yesterday's average watch: 0.8 to 6.1 s. Test: Half the Reels with the delayed second line; compare average watch time.
5. **Test one longer Reel a day (30 to 45 seconds, 4 to 5 text beats).** Evidence: One consultant's client: 12 to 15 s Reels got about 800 to 1,500 views against 11,000 for a 75 s one (Lilach Bullock, Sep 2026; an anecdote). Socialinsider showed the same direction yesterday. Test: One a day for 5 days; judge by plays and average watch.
6. **Add one still quote image a day.** Evidence: Socialinsider (Sep 2026): reach rate images 1.20% against Reels 0.55%. Meta pays on photos too. A second route to reach while Reels are stuck. Test: 5 days; compare reach and reactions with that day's Reels.
7. **Keep 'hard days' and 'the one who cares for everyone' as lead themes.** Evidence: Still the only Reels with shares or more than 4 reactions (587 plays, 20 reactions, 3 shares; 362 plays, 8 reactions). 'Permission to rest' cannot be judged: it was barely shown. Test: Already under test from Oct 13 (5-day review).
