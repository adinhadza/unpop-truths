## Patterns worth testing

- **Find out why scheduled Reels get almost no plays.** Evidence: 12 scheduled Reels (Oct 9 and 10): 35 plays, best 9. Posted straight away (Oct 6 and 8): 581, 571, 362, 259, 140. Oct 7's three, also posted straight away, got 0, so it is not proven. Test: For 3 days publish 2 of the day's Reels at once (no schedule) at the same clock times as 2 scheduled ones; compare plays after 24 hours.
- **Check whether 10 Reels a day is too many for a 7-follower page.** Evidence: Volume went from 3 a day to 8 to 10 a day on the same day plays collapsed. Socialinsider: brand pages average about 7 Reels a month; NewsWhip found no link between volume and engagement. Test: Run 3 Reels a day for 3 days, then compare median plays per Reel with Oct 9 and 10.
- **Make Reels 12 seconds or longer and aim to hold people past 5 seconds.** Evidence: Meta: videos under 10 seconds do not qualify for earnings and views under 5 seconds do not count. Our background clips are 8 to 20 seconds; average watch on our big Reels is 3.3 to 3.8 seconds. Test: Set a 12-second minimum; delay the second line to second 4 or 5 on half the Reels; compare average watch time.
- **Do not drop photo posts completely; run both for a week.** Evidence: Echobox via INMA: among about 700 paid pages, image-plus-link posts earned most and Reels very little. NewsWhip: photos were 41% of the top 1,000 publisher posts. Test: 3 Reels and 3 photo posts a day for 7 days; compare reach and engagement per post.
- **Try 30 to 45 second Reels with 2 or 3 cards instead of one 15-second card.** Evidence: Socialinsider (39M videos): 1 to 30 seconds is the weakest bucket (0.10%, about 500 views); 30 to 60 seconds gets 0.18%, about 820 views. All industries, not news. Test: Alternate lengths for 5 days; compare plays and average watch.
