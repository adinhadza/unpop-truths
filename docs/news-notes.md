# News guidance

Rules for the daily 50 State Wire build, rewritten by the 5-day review. Last rewrite: 2026-10-11.
Based on one measured Reel (98 plays, 2.7 seconds average watch) and the competitor scans; not yet proven on this page.

- Put the whole story in the first card: who, what, where readable within 2 seconds. Viewers leave after about 3.
- Open the headline with a number, an age or a US place.
- Include one animal or rescue good-news story every day, so the next review can compare it with the rest.
- Prefer US stories; use a foreign story only when it is major world news.
